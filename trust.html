Foundation

Commitment to excellence through education.

Sri Sharada Vidyamandira is bonded with commitment to excellence, under the stewardship of Sri Doddaiah, Smt Aruna Kumari Doddaiah believed in imparting three components of education — intellectual, spiritual and physical needs of students.

With this vision, the trust has started an institution White Academy.

Leadership

Managed by a board of trustees.

The trust is managed by a board of trustees and the chairman of the trust is Sri Doddaiah.

SD
Sri Doddaiah Chairman of the Trust

Initially the Trust was formed with the aim and objectives as follows

Motto: Smart Kids for bright future

01

Advance Education

To advance the cause of Education and diffusion of Knowledge without distinction of caste & creeds.

02

Start Schools

To start and run Nursery and primary schools.

03

Expand Education

To further advance the cause of education by establishing Secondary & Senior Secondary Schools for the benefit of the students of the surrounding areas.

04

Support Students

To give monetary benefits to the needy students studying in the higher classes.

White Academy

Education built on vision, values and service.

White Academy continues the trust’s mission of nurturing intellectual, spiritual and physical growth through quality education.
