Student Council

Leadership through responsibility and service.

The Student Council serves as a pivotal institution within the school community, representing student voice and promoting leadership.

Place student-council-hero.png here

Student voice with responsibility.

The Student Council represents the voice and interests of the student body. Composed of elected student representatives, the council plays a crucial role in fostering communication between students, faculty, and administration.

It acts as a platform for students to express their ideas, concerns, and aspirations, advocating for initiatives that enhance school life and promote student welfare.

By engaging in decision-making processes and implementing school-wide projects, students develop invaluable skills in organization, communication and problem-solving.
